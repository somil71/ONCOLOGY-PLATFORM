# Aggregate research figures

These PNGs contain cohort-level estimates and uncertainty intervals only; they do not contain patient identifiers or individual predictions. They summarize the verified clinical/RNA survival studies and the exploratory missing-input uncertainty audit described in [the current evidence and scope report](../../../docs/FINAL_EVIDENCE_AND_SCOPE_2026-11-19.md).

The full local run bundles and patient-level prediction exports are kept out of the public repository. The committed figure files are not evidence of four-input survival efficacy or clinical validation.
